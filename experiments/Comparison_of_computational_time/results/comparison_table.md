**Notes on metrics:**
- `max|w|` is the maximum absolute transverse deflection.
- For PINN, `final_loss` is the converged total potential energy `Π` (negative under load).
- For ODIL, `final_loss` is the converged PDE-residual L2-norm `||R_PDE||` on interior nodes.
- The two are NOT directly comparable scalars; treat them as method-specific convergence indicators.
- `wall_time_s` is end-to-end training/solve time including both linear and nonlinear sub-problems.
